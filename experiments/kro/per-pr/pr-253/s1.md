## Procedures and properties in design-post absent from design-pre

- **Shared `ensureFinalizerResource`**. Classification: **post (a)**. Preferences (correctness) require shared invariants between flat and forEach finalization paths; pre had two implementations risking drift.

## Verdict

**S1 improved.** Six (a) deletions all target dead surfaces or unread state. Three (b) absorptions consolidate duplication. Three (c) relocations move dispatch to where it belongs (concrete calls, type checks, package boundaries). Three post (a) additions encode invariants the preferences require.

Citation: this PR has the most diverse mix of S1 categories in the corpus; every move is at least one of (a)/(b)/(c)/post-(a).
